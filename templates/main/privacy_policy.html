BIO MED INGREDIENTS AUSTRALIA

Effective Date: November 2026 | Last Updated: November 2026

Bio Med Ingredients Australia ("BMI Australia", "we", "our", or "us") is dedicated to protecting the privacy, confidentiality, and security of the personal information you share with us through our digital presence. This Privacy Statement explains what information we collect, how we manage it in accordance with the Australian Privacy Principles (APPs) and applicable international data protection standards (including GDPR), and your rights concerning your personal data.

By using our website, requesting product specifications, or submitting sample inquiries, you acknowledge that you have read and understood this Privacy Statement.

1. Information We Collect

a) Information You Provide Voluntarily
  • Full name, official designation, and company name
  • Corporate contact details (email address, telephone/WhatsApp number)
  • Product inquiries, exhibition meeting bookings, and formulation requirements
  • Sample dispatch address and technical application details
  • Curriculum Vitae and professional credentials submitted for employment applications
b) Information Collected Automatically
  • IP address, approximate geographic location, and browser user agent
  • Page views, interaction duration, and referring URLs
  • Cookie preferences and device telemetry

2. How We Use Your Information

  • To provide technical specifications, Certificates of Analysis (COA), and commercial pricing
  • To coordinate and dispatch laboratory evaluation samples
  • To schedule trade exhibition and conference meetings
  • To process employment applications through our careers portal
  • To send relevant product bulletins and regulatory updates (with explicit opt-in consent)
  • To ensure digital security, prevent automated spam, and maintain operational stability

3. Data Sharing & Disclosure

We never sell, rent, or trade your personal information. Data may only be shared under the following conditions:

  • With our verified international courier partners solely for physical sample fulfillment
  • With our group manufacturing entities and accredited laboratory facilities to provide tailored technical dossiers
  • When required by statutory law, regulatory authorities, or legitimate court orders

4. Cookie Policy

Our website utilizes cookies and local storage tokens to deliver seamless browsing:

  • Strictly Necessary Cookies: Essential for session state, navigation security, and spam prevention.
  • Performance & Analytics Cookies: Anonymized metrics to evaluate catalog usage and improve performance.
  • Functional Cookies: Retains interface preferences and consent choices.

You can adjust your preferences at any time by clicking the Cookie Preferences link in our website footer.

5. Data Retention & Security

We implement enterprise-grade technical safeguards, encrypted communication protocols (HTTPS/TLS), and restricted access controls. Business inquiries are stored securely in compliance with commercial record retention guidelines.

6. Your Rights

Depending on your jurisdiction, you maintain the following rights regarding your personal information:

  • Right to request access to personal data held by us
  • Right to request correction of inaccurate records
  • Right to request deletion or restriction of processing
  • Right to unsubscribe from marketing and notification lists at any time
