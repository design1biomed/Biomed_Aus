As a leading manufacturer and exporter of herbs and botanical extracts, we always strive to provide our products and extracts only from the best ingredients nature has to offer.

Supercritical Fluid Extraction

In order to bring our customers a wider range of products and open new possibilities in the world of natural ingredients, Biomed Ingredients Australia recently opened a brand new Supercritical Fluid Extraction facility to suit the demands for high quality Oleoresins, Essential oils, and Herbal extracts.

Supercritical extraction is clean and completely solvent free. It has the ability to better extract the functional components of the ingredient therefore keeping the essential phytochemicals in the end result. Such method ensures green, environment friendly separation and concentration of active compounds by eliminating solvent residue and high temperature processing.

Our extracts, oils, and oleoresins are used by the food, nutraceutical, and cosmetic industries for enhanced flavor, fragrance, color and odor preservation, perfumery and aromatherapy.
